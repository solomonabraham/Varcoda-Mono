# Audit of version 1.000

This is the audit that preceded the 1.2.0 work. It describes the project **as it was at 1.000**, when the
family was still called Vector Mono, and what each finding led to. The 1.000 state is preserved: the four
original TTFs are in `tests/baseline/v1.000/` and are used by the before/after renders.

Everything here was read from the font files and sources, not from specimens. Measurements are in
thousandths of the em. Reference fonts (JetBrains Mono 400, Fira Code 400, Iosevka 400, taken from the
`@fontsource` npm packages, all OFL 1.1) were only measured and rendered; see `docs/COMPARISON.md`.

## 2. What already worked well

- **The grid.** One advance for all glyphs, no positioning features, ligatures that keep one cell per character.
  Verified by shaping random operator strings and by hinted advances from 8 to 32 ppem.
- **One coherent construction.** Superelliptic bowls, flat shoulders and orthogonal terminals come from one
  set of pens, so the four weights are consistent by construction.
- **Lowercase rhythm.** The variation of the white gaps between stems in `nihmulnhimun` (coefficient of
  variation) was 35 %, slightly *better* than JetBrains Mono (37 %), Fira Code (36 %) and Iosevka (39 %).
  A proposed narrowing of the lowercase was therefore **not** carried out.
- **Operator axis.** `+ - = < > ~ | /` and all brackets were centred on one line (y = 320) to the unit.
- **Look-alike separation** of 0/O, 1/l/I, 5/S, 8/B at 12–16 px, enforced by tests.
- **Engineering.** Reproducible builds, clean fontbakery run, sources that match the binaries.

## 4. What was missing

- Ligatures `:=`, `**`, `?.`, `+=`, `-=` (added and validated in 1.2.0).
- A plain zero and an alternative `l` (added as `ss03`, `ss04`).
- Powerline symbols and superscript/subscript figures (added).
- Web fonts (WOFF2 added).
- Italics, a variable font, a Nerd Font build (still missing, see `ROADMAP.md`).
- All repository scaffolding: changelog, contribution guide, issue templates, CI (added).
- Any rendering evidence from Windows or macOS (still missing).

## 5. Changes that would have damaged quality, and were not made

- **Narrowing the capitals.** Capital rhythm is a little less even than the references (CV 33 % vs 28–30 %).
  Narrowing `H` from 466 to 444 improved that to 31 % but pushed `8`/`B` and `0`/`O` below the look-alike
  threshold at 12–13 px in Bold and Light. Distinguishing characters matters more in code than capital
  rhythm, so the capitals kept their width.
- **A blanket 25 % punctuation increase.** Each mark was sized against measurements instead; the comma,
  for example, grew 21 % in width but only 9 % in height.
- **A narrower cell.** The x-height-to-advance ratio (0.90) already sits between Fira Code (0.88) and
  JetBrains Mono (0.92). A 0.5-em cell in the manner of Iosevka is a different design, not a refinement.
- **A taller line.** At 1.23 em the family is the densest of the four; accented capitals (tallest: ĥ at 936)
  and the cedilla (−196) stay inside 980/−250, so nothing is clipped and the density was kept.
- **Renumbering the stylistic sets.** `ss01` and `ss02` keep their 1.000 meaning; new sets were appended.

## 5a. A second pass: the Saira direction

After the audit the owner asked for more of Saira's character, a new name (Varcoda Mono) and details of its
own. Saira was measured the same way as the other references. Carried over as direction: squarer bowls
(squareness of `o` 0.867 → 0.893; Saira 0.910), square dots, taller ascenders. Added as the family's own:
the angled cut on extender ends. Tried and **not** made the default: Saira's u-form `y`, which fell below
the look-alike threshold against `g` (20–28 % at 12–16 px) or, with a shorter hook, against `u` (19–26 %);
it ships as `ss05`. One side effect was fixed: the squarer `O` came too close to `D` (14 % of ink in one
weight, threshold 15 %), so `D` got tighter corners.

## 6. Recommended priorities (as decided at audit time)

1. Punctuation, brackets and asterisk sizes (largest measured gap, cheap, low risk). **Done.**
2. Regular weight and the missing SemiBold. **Done.**
3. `j` and `r` side bearings. **Done.**
4. Requested ligatures and alternates, with shaping tests. **Done.**
5. Terminal symbols that need no third-party glyphs (Powerline). **Done.**
6. Repository scaffolding, CI, assets. **Done locally; nothing has been published.**
7. Variable font. **Investigated, not shipped:** 97 of 647 glyphs change their point structure between
   weights (`python scripts/check-interpolation.py --list`).
8. Italics. **Not started.** A sheared roman would not meet the bar, a drawn italic is a release of its own.
9. Rendering checks on Windows and macOS. **Open**; needs real machines.
